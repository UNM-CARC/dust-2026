OPEN SCIENCE FOLLOWS THE MONEY

REQUIRED

Public access at acceptance

NIH: every manuscript accepted on or after 1 July 2025 goes to PubMed Central with no embargo. DOE, EPA, USGS, NSF and USDA have equivalent policies.

FEDERAL FRAME

Gold Standard Science

Executive Order 14303 (23 May 2025) sets nine tenets. Agencies filed plans in August 2025 and first annual reports on 1 September 2026.

EXPECTED

Communities

People near Arizona-Sonora mine tailings, and Navajo Nation and Pueblo of Laguna communities near abandoned uranium mines, expect results shared with them.

SRP EXAMPLE: WHY IT MATTERS HERE

ARIZONA

Arsenic in tailings dust

Findings about arsenic in mine-tailings dust and lung injury protect people only if health officials and residents can read them promptly: papers public at acceptance, protocols reproducible.

DEFINITION · UNESCO RECOMMENDATION ON OPEN SCIENCE

“Open Science is defined as an inclusive construct that combines various movements and practices aiming to make multilingual scientific knowledge openly available, accessible and reusable for everyone.”

Sharing the papers, data, methods, code and teaching materials from research, so anyone can check, use and build on them.

THE SIX PILLARS

Open access

Free to read. NIH is satisfied by the free accepted manuscript in PubMed Central; paid gold OA is optional.

Open data

Deposited with a persistent identifier, FAIR, within limits set by privacy, safety and CARE.

Open educational resources

Teaching and training materials under an open license such as CC BY.

Open methodology

Protocols, version-controlled code and pre-registered hypotheses and analysis plans.

Open peer review

Reviews signed, published or both; preprints reviewed before journal submission.

Open source software

Research software public under a recognized open license.

TWO THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE YOU CITE IT

WHAT NIH COMMITS TO

Training, compliance, replication

NIH's 22 August 2025 plan: more rigor and reproducibility training, stronger data-sharing compliance, support for replication studies, periodic public reporting.

1 · MOSTLY FAMILIAR

Partly new

Reviews (AIP FYI, SPARC) find the plans largely restate existing policy. New: annual reporting, AI tools to check reproducibility and bias, oversight of all funded research.

2 · CONTESTED

Follow the debate

The order gives political appointees a role in judging scientific integrity; critics in Science and SPARC warn this could constrain inquiry. Practice the tenets on their merits.

DUST 2026 · LESSON 1 · OPEN SCIENCE

THREE FACTS FOR THIS YEAR

1 · AT ACCEPTANCE

Manuscript to PMC

Deposit the free accepted manuscript in PubMed Central at acceptance, no embargo. That satisfies NIH. No APC is required.

2 · IN THE BUDGET

Publication costs

NIH floated APC caps (NOT-OD-25-138). OMB's 29 May 2026 proposal would make publication costs unallowable unless pre-approved. Budget them explicitly.

3 · AS CLOSED AS NECESSARY

CARE and tribal review

Navajo Nation data need NNHRRB approval before collection and before release; Pueblo of Laguna has its own review. CARE governs.

DUST 2026 · LESSON 1 · OPEN SCIENCE

THE APC YOU NEED TO PAY TO COMPLY WITH NIH

$0

Nature's 2026 list price for open access is $12,850. The free accepted manuscript in PubMed Central is enough.
