# Lessons

Three 50-minute lessons, taken in order, move from the principles of open science to managing research data and to using artificial intelligence responsibly. Each lesson follows the same structure: introduction (5 min), core concepts (25 min), hands-on activity (15 min), and wrap-up (5 min).

* [Lesson 1: Foundations of Open Science](01-open-science.md) - What open science is, its six pillars, why it matters for Superfund research in Arizona and New Mexico, and the 2026 US public-access and publication-cost landscape.
* [Lesson 2: Modern Data Management for Computational Research](02-data-management.md) - The data life cycle, FAIR and CARE principles, the 2026 NIH and NSF data management and sharing plan formats, data rescue, and a two-site metal-mixture DMP exercise.
* [Lesson 3: Ethics and Artificial Intelligence](03-ai-ethics.md) - Sources of AI bias, responsible and agentic AI use in research, NIH and journal AI rules, energy and water costs, the 2026 regulatory landscape, and six discussion scenarios.
